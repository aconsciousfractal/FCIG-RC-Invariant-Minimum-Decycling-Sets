\section{Finite validation boundaries}
\label{app:finite}

The finite validation summarized in Table~\ref{tab:validation} has four
epistemic roles:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item verify the left-rotation and gradient conventions;
  \item exercise negative-ray, strict-crossing, and zero-sector cases;
  \item replay every legal firing and terminal invariant in the declared
  finite panel;
  \item reject mutations that would otherwise look like harmless convention
  changes.
\end{enumerate}
It does not establish a recurrence, interpolate in $k$, or replace any
displayed lemma.  Runtime, file-envelope, and platform hashes are deliberately
kept separate from the stable scientific payload digest.
