\section{Finite validation boundaries}
\label{app:finite}

The executable binary panel has four epistemic roles: it verifies the
left-rotation and gradient conventions; exercises negative-ray,
strict-crossing, and zero-sector cases; replays every legal firing and
terminal invariant; and rejects convention mutations.  It neither
interpolates in $k$ nor tests the alphabet-general theorem.
