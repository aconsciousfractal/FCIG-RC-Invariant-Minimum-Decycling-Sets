\section{Exact validation and reproducibility}
\label{sec:validation}

An independent finite validation was used to audit the convention map, not
to infer the theorem.  Two implementations reconstructed the selector,
zero ties, residual DAG, edgewise coboundary identity, firing path, and
terminal RC-fixed MDS with exact cyclotomic arithmetic at
$k=2,4,6,8,10,12$.  One implementation generated coordinates from an
integer recurrence in $\Z[2\cos(2\pi/k)]$; the other used independent frozen
coordinate tables and a separate recursive DFS DAG oracle.
