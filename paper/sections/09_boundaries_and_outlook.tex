\section{Boundaries and outlook}
\label{sec:boundaries}

The result isolates a sharp parity effect for literal reverse-complement
invariance at the ordinary binary necklace bound.  Several nearby questions
remain outside its scope.

\begin{itemize}
  \item The odd proof gives $\Delta_2(k)\geq2$ but no exact formula for the
  symmetry premium.
  \item The even construction is binary.  Although the odd obstruction is
  alphabet-general, an even construction for larger fixed-point-free
  complement alphabets would require a separate argument.
  \item The firing proof supplies termination but no optimized step count or
  algorithmic complexity theorem.
  \item No biological, empirical sketching-performance, or clinical claim
  follows from this existence theorem.
\end{itemize}

The direct predecessor literature establishes ordinary MDS constructions,
move structure, and the reverse-complement-constrained design problem
\cite{Mykkeltveit1972,ChamparnaudHanselPerrin2004,MarcaisDeBlasioKingsford2024,MarcaisElderKingsford2024}.
We are not aware of a direct later proof of the same binary parity statement.
Our targeted primary-source search was not logged at exhaustive-query level;
it therefore supplies neither a priority claim nor a claim of literature
exhaustion.  Any future novelty assessment must remain within that documented
search boundary.

One conceptual lesson of the proof is that exact equivariance need not be
present in the classical seed itself.  It can instead appear as an integral
coboundary with a nonnegative, symmetry-invariant potential, provided the
ambient move system realizes every positive defect by a legal descent.  The
odd bridge shows why no analogous minimum construction can cross the parity
boundary.
