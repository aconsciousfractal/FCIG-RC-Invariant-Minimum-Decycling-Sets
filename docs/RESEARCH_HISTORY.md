# Curated Research History

The project began from the known ordinary minimum decycling-set problem and a
reverse-complement symmetry constraint. The odd obstruction was isolated
early: a primitive rotation cycle links two RC-fixed lower states, so an
RC-invariant minimum deletion cannot destroy both directed endpoint routes.

The even direction required a uniform construction. Several natural ideas
were investigated and rejected or classified as equivalent reformulations,
including direct symmetry averaging, local selector symmetrization, bounded
potential ansatzes, component-parity shortcuts, recursive endpoint cylinders,
plateau controllers and progressively larger finite closures. Those negative
results informed the final proof but are not prerequisites for it.

The successful route returns to the classical cyclotomic selector. Arbitrary
choices on zero-embedding PCRs are proved acyclic, so those ties may be chosen
RC-equivariantly. The resulting seed has an exact RC defect equal to the
gradient of an open-upper-half-plane indicator on lower states. A positive
potential lemma then supplies a legal source firing, and each firing lowers a
nonnegative symmetric integer potential in two coordinates. Termination gives
an RC-fixed ordinary MDS at every even order.

An exact finite panel through `k=12` was built only after the proof route was
identified. Its purpose is to falsify sign, ray, tie, action and update errors.
The final repository contains the proof-facing evidence needed to inspect and
reproduce that panel; exploratory material unrelated to the theorem is outside
its scope.
