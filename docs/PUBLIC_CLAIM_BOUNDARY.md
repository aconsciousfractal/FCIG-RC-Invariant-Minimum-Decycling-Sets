# Public Claim Boundary

## Intended mathematical claim

> For every binary `k >= 2`, an RC-invariant ordinary minimum decycling set in
> the directed binary de Bruijn graph `B(2,k)` exists if and only if `k` is
> even.

The odd nonexistence argument is stated for every finite alphabet with a
fixed-point-free complement.  The positive even construction is binary.

If `tau_RC(k)` denotes the minimum cardinality under literal RC invariance,
the symmetry premium is `Delta_2(k)=tau_RC(k)-N_2(k)`.  The paper proves
`Delta_2(k)=0` for even `k` and `Delta_2(k)>=2` for odd `k`; it gives no exact
odd formula.

## Not claimed

- an even-order theorem for larger alphabets;
- an exact formula for the odd symmetry premium;
- a shortest firing sequence or complexity bound;
- biological, genomic, sketching-performance, empirical, or clinical value;
- novelty or priority over every unlogged source;
- that finite verification proves an all-order statement;
- that one-per-PCR selection alone implies decycling;
- that repository distribution alone establishes journal publication,
  formal publication status, or bibliographic priority.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. The paper's written proof is authority for the all-order theorem.
2. The standalone derivation preserves detailed mathematical provenance.
3. The two independent implementations provide exact finite convention and
   mutation assurance only at `k=2,4,6,8,10,12`.
4. Stored hashes establish artifact identity, not mathematical truth.

The bounded primary-source search found no direct collision with the theorem,
but it does not establish novelty or exhaustive priority.
