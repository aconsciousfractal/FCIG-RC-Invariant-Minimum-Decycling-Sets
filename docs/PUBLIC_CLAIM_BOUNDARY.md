# Public Claim Boundary

## Mathematical claims

Let `A` be a finite nonempty alphabet with a fixed-point-free involutive
complement and `q=|A|`.

1. For every integer `k>=2`, an RC-invariant ordinary minimum decycling set
   in `B(q,k)` exists if and only if `k` is even.
2. At even `k`, the invariant minimum has ordinary cardinality `N_q(k)`.
3. At odd `k>=3`, the symmetry-constrained minimum exceeds `N_q(k)` by at
   least two.
4. Every injective nonzero real weight map satisfying
   `s(bar(a))=-s(a)` supports the geometric seed, legal descent, and direct
   selector at even `k>=4`; integrality is not an existence hypothesis.
5. The exhibited monotone defect descent uses exactly
   `|{u: lambda_s(u)>0}|/2` physical full-alphabet firings.
6. For an integer weight table given in binary, a self-dual spectral ideal
   gives a deterministic direct selector whose per-word membership is
   decidable with polynomial bit complexity and no exponentially sized
   table.

The binary alphabet and DNA alphabet are literal special cases. The theorem
starts at `k=2`; at `k=1` the unique ordinary minimum is the whole alphabet.

## Not claimed

- an exact formula for the odd symmetry premium;
- a unique terminal, confluence, or shortest firing path;
- a bound on the longest residual directed path or a competitive window
  guarantee;
- polynomial total time to list an exponentially large MDS;
- polynomial-bit computation for an arbitrary real weight table lacking a
  finite exact representation;
- biological, genomic, sketching-performance, runtime, memory, clinical, or
  production-software value;
- novelty or priority over every unexamined source;
- that finite verification proves an all-order theorem;
- that one-per-PCR selection alone implies decycling;
- journal publication, peer review, or bibliographic priority merely from
  repository distribution.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. The paper's written proof is authority for the all-order theorem.
2. The standalone derivation records the same q-ary proof in audit-friendly
   form.
3. The two exact implementations check only the binary convention map at
   `k=2,4,6,8,10,12`.
4. Stored hashes establish artifact identity, not mathematical truth or
   priority.

The scoped source search found no direct statement of the weighted q-ary
parity theorem or spectral midpoint selector. This is a source-search result,
not an exhaustive novelty or freedom-to-operate opinion.
